Climate and Agriculture in Pakistan

Interactive companion to the EDA notebook. By Sumbal Fatima, BSc Agricultural Engineering, University of Agriculture, Faisalabad.

Exploratory analysis of public Kaggle data. Trend tests show whether a series changes, not why. Hover over charts for values and use the menus to switch views.

1. Temperature trend

Pick a start year. Orange dashes are the Sen slope. p-values are autocorrelation-adjusted (Hamed-Rao). Early years before 1870 are excluded as unreliable.

2. Crop yields, Pakistan vs India

Choose a crop. Maize jumped after about 2002, so a straight line fits it poorly.

3. Wheat yield ranking

Pakistan in orange, India and Egypt in blue.

4. Record climate extremes

Record highs (solid) and lows (dashed) for each month.

Data: Kaggle (Tayyar Hussain; patelris Crop Yield Prediction Dataset, from FAO and World Bank data).
